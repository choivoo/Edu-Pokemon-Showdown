# Pokémon Quest Fan Edition 2.1.0 — PokéAPI 2D Dot Upgrade

An unofficial, noncommercial 2D fan-game update. Everything remains 2D.

**New default artwork:** Pokémon sprites are requested from the PokéAPI/sprites public image repository, particularly the Generation V Black & White static pixel sprites (front/back). If an image fails, the game falls back to its original self-generated 2D Canvas Pokémon art.

**New or fixed gameplay:** Reviving after 25 seconds with surviving teammates; optional second attack move with independent cooldown and move stone count; auto-evolution at level thresholds, Everstone switch and stone-dependent Eevee evolution; independent bingo perks at 3/6/9 stones; newly dropped numeric Attack and HP Power Stones; free PM ticket member service every 22 hours, quest ticket rewards, purchasable 2D base-camp decorations with boosts to ingredient drops, training, recycling, and battery; stone recycling; current and legacy encrypted save key support.

**Preserved gameplay:** 54 original-inspired main island expeditions across eleven worlds, nine five-minute Happenstance Island postgame expeditions, the original fan-created 30-stage sequel, 100 scene diary, 154 Pokémon data records, original synthetic music.

**Accuracy and rights:** This is not a guaranteed 1:1 reproduction of every Pokémon Quest move, numeric stat, random enemy wave, original audio, 3D model, scripted cutscene or premium decoration. The content in `PARITY_AUDIT_2D.md` lists concrete remaining differences. Pokémon and its sprites belong to the rights holders; PokéAPI hosting does not grant commercial redistribution rights or imply official approval. The game remains unofficial and noncommercial.
