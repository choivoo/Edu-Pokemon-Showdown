# Pokémon Quest: Fan Edition 2.0.0 — 2D Adventure Edition

**Independent 2D adaptation of the full main island campaign + postgame + original fan sequel** (unofficial, non-commercial fan project).

### Playable campaign
- Original island adventure: 11 worlds, 54 expeditions, ten earned statues, MoBee mystery, Mewtwo ending.
- Original postgame: Happenstance Island, nine independently selectable 5-minute expeditions, random opposing bosses and environments, Mystical Shell drops, final legendary trial.
- Fan sequel: six extra regions, 30 stages, 46 additional story scenes, four epilogues, separate endless challenge mode. Previous saves still open existing fan progress.
- Story journal: 54 main-island reconstruction scenes plus 46 fan-sequel scenes.

### Core 2D RPG systems
- 154 data entries including 151 Kanto Pokémon. Self-drawn runtime Canvas cube-inspired sprite system (no extracted retail game 3D assets).
- Five selectable starter Pokémon, three active team slots, level cap 100, level training, move training, party auto-attack and manual moves.
- Nine-slot 3×3 Power Charm with attack and HP Power Stones for newly obtained gear, progressive unlocks and bingo bonuses. Historical custom multi-shape stones remain in old saves.
- Four cooking-pot types unlocked through expeditions, different ingredient quantities and cook timers. Mystical Shell cooking unlocks Articuno/Zapdos/Moltres/Mewtwo/Mew.
- Type-based combat bonuses, six support stones, status effects and scatter command; original 10 statues provide 1.5× expedition Exp to Pokémon under a growing level cap.
- Fully local browser saves, encrypted import/export keys, responsive landscape play; 12 original synthesized music pieces and four jingles.

### Scope transparency
This is a **major playable fan release**, not a verified exact 1:1 recreation. Detailed original move pools and battle physics, every unique enemy spawn/encounter rate, Power Stone numeric attributes and all decorations, complete animated original cutscenes, and pixel-perfect matching to the retail game are not represented as identical. Existing 3D retail artwork/music/dialogue is not included. A prior v1 multi-cell stone can remain as a legacy item for save compatibility, though new drops use one cell.
